LifeLink connects donors, blood banks, hospitals and patients in one intelligent network — finding blood faster, predicting shortages, and coordinating emergencies in real time.

The Problem

🏝️
Isolated Banks

Blood banks operate without shared visibility into nearby stock.

⏳
Expiry Waste

Usable units expire unused while other facilities face shortages.

🕑
Slow Emergencies

Coordination during critical requests takes hours, not minutes.

❓
No Prediction

Shortages are discovered only after they already happen.
